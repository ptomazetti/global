Questions before your viewing

Which rental guarantees do you accept?

Rent guarantee insurance (seguro fiança), a deposit of 3 months’ rent (caução) or a guarantor (fiador).

Does the rent include the condo fee and IPTU tax?

No. The condo fee and IPTU property tax are charged separately. Each apartment shows all three amounts and the monthly total, so you can compare with no surprises.

What exactly comes with the apartment?

Each apartment has a full description listing the furniture and appliances. Tap "Description" on the apartment card. The description is in Portuguese.

Is the apartment cleaned before I move in?

Yes. Before every new tenant, the apartment is repainted and deep cleaned.

How do I book a viewing?

On WhatsApp at +55 (15) 99747-0301. Tell us which apartment and the best day and time for you. You can also email contato@globalestadia.com.br.

Is there parking?

Spettacolo Patriani has 2 covered parking spaces and a 10 m² storage room. Alpha Club I41 and Residencial Esplanada have 1 space each. For the other apartments, please confirm on WhatsApp.

Do the apartments have air conditioning?

Alpha Club J42 has 12,000 BTU air conditioning in the master bedroom. Residencial Esplanada has 12,000 BTU air conditioning in the living room. Alpha Club I41 has a ceiling fan in the master bedroom.

Do the buildings have leisure facilities?

Yes. Spettacolo Patriani has a lap pool, spa with hot tub, sauna, gym, Pilates studio, party room, tennis court and kids’ playroom. Alpha Club and Residencial Esplanada are club-style complexes, and Vida Plena has full leisure facilities.

I am a real estate agent. Can I bring my client to visit?

Yes. Send everything in one message: name, ID (RG), CRECI licence, apartment, and preferred day and time. The button in the Agents section opens WhatsApp with these fields ready.

I need a short stay. Do you offer that?

Yes. For short stays, look for Global Flats on Airbnb.
