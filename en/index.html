Questions before your viewing

How long is the lease, and which guarantees do you accept?

The lease is 30 months, and after 12 months you can leave without a penalty. We accept rent guarantee insurance (seguro fiança), a deposit of 3 months’ rent (caução) or a guarantor (fiador).

What does the price include?

The rent. The condo fee and IPTU tax are charged separately, and each apartment shows the monthly total. Electricity and internet go in the tenant’s name, as do water and gas when they are not in the condo fee. Exception: at RED Studio, 500 Mbps internet is already included.

What comes with the apartment?

Furniture, an equipped kitchen and appliances. The list for each one is under "Photos and details". Before every new tenant, the apartment is repainted and deep cleaned.

Are pets allowed?

Yes.

Is there parking, air conditioning and leisure?

Parking: Spettacolo Patriani has 2 covered spaces and a 10 m² storage room, RED Studio has 2 covered spaces, and Alpha Club I41 and Residencial Esplanada have 1 each. For the others, please confirm on WhatsApp. Air conditioning: Alpha Club J42 in the master bedroom and Residencial Esplanada in the living room. Alpha Club I41 has a ceiling fan in the master bedroom. All buildings have leisure facilities.

How do I book a viewing, and how soon do I get the keys?

On WhatsApp: tell us the apartment, day and time. You can also email contato@globalestadia.com.br. Agents: include your name, ID (RG) and CRECI in the same message. Keys are ready within 2 days of receiving your documents.
